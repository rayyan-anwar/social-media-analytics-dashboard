# System Card

Author: Rayyan Anwar

**System:** descriptive analytics over user-supplied CSV exports of posts. No machine-learned model; sentiment is a small English lexicon with negation.

**Intended use:** exploring your own or properly licensed post exports; learning dashboard design.

**Out of scope / prohibited:** profiling or surveilling individuals, inferring personal attributes, scoring people, automated moderation or enforcement, claims about causes of engagement, forecasting.

**Data and privacy:** uploads are processed in memory and not stored; do not upload data you lack the right to process. Post text can contain personal data. Sample data is synthetic.

**Risks and limitations:** lexicon sentiment is crude and English-only; trend and spike flags are heuristics; best-time estimates ignore content and audience; engagement weights are equal; platform APIs and terms of service are not integrated (no scraping or live connectors).
